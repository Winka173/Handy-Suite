## English

Handy Trip - Plan, visit, remember

An offline travel daybook for itineraries, saved places, packing, bookings and
expenses. Confirm your visits, keep original photos and a journal, create personal
stamps and export a Memory Book. Back up your library with portable JSON/ZIP.
No account, cloud AI, background location tracking or paid map subscription.
Maps open externally only when requested. Stamps are personal souvenirs,
not official travel or immigration documents. Optional Pro Lifetime adds stamp/
book styles and removes ads where supported.
