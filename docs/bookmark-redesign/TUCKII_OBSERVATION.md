## The first run, step by step (as recorded)

A ten-segment progress bar runs across the top; the current segment is the brand yellow.
There is a back arrow from step 2, and **Skip** appears only from step 4 (the practice).
Every step stages its content in the same order: headline, then a marker highlight drawn
under its key words, then the illustration, and **the button last**. The button first shows
pale, then fills, about 1–1.5 s after the step opens, so nobody taps through a beat unseen.

## What is worth learning (behaviour, not look)

1. **A story in three beats before any instruction:** the problem (scattered), its cost
   (never opened), the answer. Each beat is one sentence and one moving picture.
2. **The button comes last.** Pacing is enforced gently, so the picture is seen.
3. **Rehearse the real gesture.** The person practises Share → app → collection → save on a
   safe mock, with one spotlight at a time and a written cue next to it.
4. **Show the result, then name what was done for them** ("filled in for you").
5. **Teach the habit-forming feature by doing it once** (three-card Focus deck).
6. **Ask for notifications with a picture of the notification**, with a plain "Maybe later".
7. Motion is continuity: the splash becomes the card, the card becomes the pile, the sheet
   becomes the check. Nothing moves only to decorate.

What we will not do: the mascot, the sticker style (black outlines, hard offset shadows,
yellow marker), their wording, a paywall inside the first run, a free plan capped per day,
a practice that throws away what the person did, or Skip hidden for the first three
screens. Handy Bookmark has no subscription; Pro is a one-time Lifetime purchase offered in
Settings, never in the welcome.
