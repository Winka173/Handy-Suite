You save a reel in Facebook, a video in TikTok, a post on X, take a screenshot… and never find it again. Handy Bookmark keeps them in one place, with their pictures, so you come back to them.

SAVE FROM ANY APP
• Tap Share in Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, Threads, Reddit or your browser, then Handy Bookmark.
• It is saved at once. A small sheet shows its picture and title: tap a collection and it goes there, or name a new one on the spot.
• Your collections also appear in the top row of Android's share sheet for one-tap saving.
• Paste a link in the app, or add screenshots and photos.

TITLES AND PICTURES, BY THEMSELVES
• Each link gets the page's own title and picture: reels, videos, posts and articles.
• No more lists of bare addresses.

COLLECTIONS THAT LOOK LIKE YOURS
• Collections with their own colour, icon or emoji, inside one another if you like.
• Everything new waits in Not sorted until you put it somewhere.

FIND IT AGAIN
• Search titles, notes, sites and collections.
• Focus shows what you saved one at a time, so things actually get looked at.
• Reminders bring a link back on the day you choose.

YOURS, ON YOUR PHONE
• No account and no sign-in. Your library is kept on your phone.
• Back up to a file, protect a backup with a password, or keep automatic backups in a folder you choose.
• Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.

To show a link's title and picture, the app asks that page's own site; this can be switched off in Settings.
