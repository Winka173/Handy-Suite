Work is everywhere: a line in your notes, a message in the class group, a scrap of paper on the desk… and the deadline arrives before you remember. Handy Boards brings it all onto one board: columns say where each piece of work is, colours say what it belongs to.

KANBAN THAT STAYS SIMPLE
• Each card is one piece of work. Drag it from To do to Doing to Done.
• On a phone each column fills the screen; swipe to the next. On a computer the columns sit side by side.
• Rather not drag? Use Move to… Everything can be undone.
• Name columns your way, add more, mark the finished one.

CATEGORIES THAT FOLLOW THE CARD
• Maths, Literature, Home, Design…: each category has its colour and stays with the card in every column.
• The category strip shows how much of each is done. Tap one to see only its cards.

SUBTASKS, DUE DATES, REMINDERS
• Break a card into small steps and tick them off.
• Set a due date and a reminder. Home shows what is due today and what is overdue.
• See it by time: which card falls on which day, from today to three months. Drag a card onto a day to set its date.

A QUICK START
• A blank three-column board, or a ready template: school term, personal, shopping, project.
• See the whole template board before you use it.
• Each board gets its own cover and colour.

LIGHT AND PRIVATE
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
• Back up to one file, automatic daily copies, Trash and archive to get things back.
• Export a board as a picture or PDF; export CSV, Markdown or an ICS calendar.
• A home-screen widget for what is due today.
• English and Vietnamese, light and dark.

Five more templates, every cover, five board looks, repeating cards, small automations, poster and printable exports, and board insights. No subscription. Your boards are always free.
